Plots use the selected saved frames without smoothing.
CSV values and physical units are unchanged.
Radial density is an equal-frame mean; shading is across-frame SD.
Radial slip curves pool particle samples, weighting squared RMS by count.
These are finite-gap slip proxies, not extrapolated surface slip lengths.
Nearest-neighbor curves require --nearest-neighbors during CSV generation.
Heatmap cells represent snapshot samples, not resolved between-frame dynamics.
